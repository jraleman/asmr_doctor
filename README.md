# ASMR Doctor
 
A cozy, soothing simulation where you run a tiny, magical clinic that treats stress, tension, and restless minds through whispers, gentle sounds, and tactile care.
 
Patients arrive frazzled, from a burned-out office worker to a jittery knight to a grumpy cloud. You calm them with a soft voice and an array of satisfying tools. The tone is warm, dreamy, and quietly funny, built to relax the player as much as the patients.
 
---
 
## Concept
 
Run a small clinic of calm. Listen closely, speak softly, and discover what soothes each patient. The **microphone** is the main controller: whispers, soft taps, and breathy sounds are all part of the treatment. Mouse and keyboard fallback is always available for players who prefer silence.
 
---
 
## Core Mechanics
 
### Whisper Input
The game reads the player's soft voice, pacing, and breath. Speaking gently and steadily soothes patients, while a sudden loud noise makes them flinch.
 
### Treatment Stations
Tapping, brushing, page-turning, crinkling, and scissor-snipping each have their own tools and rhythmic mini-games, all driven by satisfying, high-quality sound design.
 
### Calm Meter
Each patient has a stress level that drops as you find the right sounds and pace. Visual feedback includes fading tension lines, softening colors, and drooping shoulders.
 
### Trigger Matching
Every patient responds to different triggers. Listen to their reactions (a sigh, a shiver, a smile) and discover what works for each one.

## Patients and Quirks
 
- Burned-out professionals, nervous first-timers, grumpy retirees, and sleepy kids who refuse to nap
- Fantastical visitors like a dragon with itchy scales, a ghost who can't relax, or a robot with rusty nerves
- Hidden preferences and funny personalities, such as the patient who only relaxes to the sound of a stapler
- Regulars who return and share how their lives are improving, with simple heartwarming story arcs
---
 
## Clinic and Atmosphere
 
- **Cozy clinic design:** Decorate the waiting room, treatment rooms, and reception with lamps, plants, rugs, and soft lighting.
- **Ambient soundscapes:** Rain on the window, a ticking clock, a distant fireplace, all adjustable to set the mood.
- **Day and night shifts:** Daytime brings busy appointments, while the midnight shift offers sleepless patients and dreamier treatments.
- **Tool collection:** Unlock feather brushes, wooden combs, glass bottles, mic windscreens, silk cloths, and other tools with unique sounds.
---
 
## Progression and Variety
 
- **Reputation system:** Happy patients spread the word, and the clinic grows from a quiet corner room to a legendary sanctuary.
- **Specialist wings:** Unlock themed rooms like the Sleep Suite, the Tingle Lab, and the Rain Room.
- **Skill growth:** Learn techniques like Slow Breath, Soft Tap Combo, and Whisper Lullaby, with new ones earned through practice.
- **Gentle challenges:** Handle sensitive patients who dislike certain sounds, or manage several appointments in a row without breaking the calm.
---
 
## Extras
 
- **Patient journal:** Track every patient's favorite triggers, quirks, and progress.
- **Sleep mode:** A relaxation mode with endless soundscapes and no goals, perfect for winding down.
- **Custom playlists:** Build and save your own calming soundscapes to share.
- **Photo and sound capture:** Save peaceful clinic moments, with optional recordings of your favorite treatments.
- **A gentle narrator** whose soothing voice walks you through each appointment.
---
 
## Controls
 
| Input | Action |
|-------|--------|
| Microphone (whisper) | Speak soothingly to patients |
| Microphone (soft taps, breath) | Perform sound-based treatments |
| Mouse | Select tools, interact with stations, decorate the clinic |
| Keyboard | Fallback for all microphone actions |
 
---
 
## Accessibility
 
- The microphone is an optional enhancement, not a requirement.
- Full mouse and keyboard fallback for every treatment.
- Adjustable volume, sound intensity, and visual contrast.
- Sensitive-sound options so players can avoid sounds they find uncomfortable.
---
 
## Design Pillars
 
1. **Calm first:** Every system should lower tension, never raise it.
2. **Sound is the star:** High-quality audio drives every interaction.
3. **Quiet charm:** Humor and heart stay gentle, never loud.
4. **Play your way:** Microphone, mouse, or keyboard, all are welcome.
